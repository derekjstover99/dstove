A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Let’s clear things up.

How much does a coating project cost?

Pricing depends on square footage, the condition of the concrete, repairs, existing coating removal, and your chosen system. Request a free estimate for a project-specific quote.

Can my floor be finished in one day?

One-day installation may be an option for qualifying projects. Preparation, weather, moisture, and the selected products determine installation and cure times. We’ll confirm the schedule for your project.

Can you coat cracked or previously painted concrete?

We assess existing coatings and damage first. Removal or repairs may be needed, and some slab conditions require additional work before a coating is suitable.

How do I choose a finish?

We’ll discuss appearance, texture, exposure, and how you use the space. Ask about decorative flake finishes and suitable coating options during your estimate.
